# Sources: photography

Added on 3 November 2026 at the user's request. Read `sources-major-houses.md` and
`sources-press.md` alongside it; for fine art photography also read
`sources-art.md`.

**Scope.** Photographs (vintage and modern prints, daguerreotypes and early
processes), photobooks, and cameras and photographic equipment. A realised price
must come from a page you have fetched in this session.

## Verification notes

A vintage print (made at the time) and a later print of the same negative are
different objects with different prices: state which the lot says it is, its
date and the edition. For cameras, state the model, serial number and condition
as the lot gives them.
