# Sources: sports memorabilia

Added on 3 November 2026 at the user's request. `sources-trading-cards.md` covers
cards, not game-used items. Read `sources-major-houses.md` and `sources-press.md`
alongside it. For cards and graded items also read `sources-trading-cards.md`;
for signed items also read `sources-autographs.md`.

**Scope.** Game-used and game-worn items, trophies, medals, signed sports items and
sporting artifacts. Trading cards belong to the cards file. A realised price must
come from a page you have fetched in this session.

**Access.** Measured with an unattended fetch on 3 November 2026; none has been
opened and logged yet. Record here what opens and what basis each
page states.

## Verification notes

A game-used claim is the price: state whether the house calls the item game-used,
game-worn or team-issued, and who authenticated it (for example MEARS, PSA/DNA,
Resolution Photomatching) exactly as the lot states it. A private sale is never a
record at auction: log it as context with the route.
