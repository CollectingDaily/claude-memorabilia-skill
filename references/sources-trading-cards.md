## Verification notes for trading cards

**Grade is the price, and the grader matters.** A PSA 10 and a BGS 9.5 are not
equivalent, and neither converts to the other. Always give the number, the
grading company, and any qualifier. A card described only as "mint" is not a
usable record.

**Population reports are a snapshot.** "One of only 3 PSA 10s" changes as more
cards are submitted. Quote the figure with the date you checked it and name the
grader, or omit it.

**Parallels, inserts and print runs.** A base card, a numbered parallel and a
1/1 patch card sharing a player and a year are different objects. Record the set,
the parallel, and the serial number where there is one.

**Raw versus graded.** Never compare an ungraded sale with a graded one, and
never present a raw sale as evidence of a graded card's value.

**Private and brokered sales.** Several platforms report private treaty sales
alongside auctions. Say which a figure is. A brokered private sale is not an
auction result.
