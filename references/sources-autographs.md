# Sources: autographs and historical documents

Added on 3 November 2026 at the user's request. Read `sources-major-houses.md`
and `sources-press.md` alongside it, exactly as for any other field.

**Scope.** Hand-signed items by named signers: letters, documents, photographs,
books and signed memorabilia. A realised price must come from a page you have
fetched in this session. A signed item counts only where the signature is what the
house sells it for.

**Access.** None of the houses below has been opened and logged yet except where
stated. On the first article that uses one, record here what opened (unattended, or only
in the user's Chrome) and whether the page states hammer or total with premium.

## Verification notes

A signed letter, a signed document and a signed photograph of the same person
differ by orders of magnitude: state the object type, whether the whole text is in
the signer's hand (ALS, TLS, DS) and the authenticator, exactly as the lot gives
them. A machine or secretarial signature is not an autograph.
