# Sources: space and aviation

Added on 3 November 2026 at the user's request. Read `sources-major-houses.md` and
`sources-press.md` alongside it, and `sources-autographs.md` for astronaut and
aviator signatures.

**Scope.** Flown and mission artifacts, astronaut and crew items, spacecraft and
rocket hardware, space photographs, and historic aviation: flying clothing,
logbooks, aircraft parts, aviators' papers and medals. A realised price must come
from a page you have fetched in this session.

## Primary authorised sources: realised prices

- **RR Auction**, https://www.rrauction.com/departments/space-exploration/. Read
  on 3 November 2026: "In addition to dedicated space sections in each of our
  monthly auctions, we host at least two Space and Aviation sales each year." It
  lists flown artifacts, astronaut autographs, rocket hardware, vintage photographs
  and meteorites. Access: 403 to an unattended fetch; opened in the in-app browser.
- **Dominic Winter**, https://www.dominicwinter.co.uk/military-aviation-history/.
  Read on 3 November 2026: "specialist auctions of Historic Aviation, Transport,
  Arms, Medals and Militaria for over 20 years", in biannual spring and autumn
  sales, including aviation collectables and logbooks. Access: an unattended fetch
  returned 202 with no page; opened in the in-app browser.
- **C&T Auctioneers**, https://www.candtauctions.co.uk/specialist-departments/arms-armour-militaria/military-aviation/.
  Read on 3 November 2026: aviation history "from all periods", including flying
  clothing, medal groups, log books and aircraft parts; the page lists recent
  results as "Sold for £...". Opened unattended.

## Majors

Heritage, Sotheby's, Bonhams and Christie's from `sources-major-houses.md`. Their

## Press and news (context only, never a price)

- **collectSPACE**, https://www.collectspace.com/. A space history news site
  ("where space exploration meets pop culture"), not a house. Opened unattended.

## Verification notes

"Flown" is the price: state exactly what the lot says flew (to the Moon, in lunar
orbit, aboard which mission) and who certifies it. A crew-signed item is not a
flown item.
