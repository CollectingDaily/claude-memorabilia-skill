## Verification notes for music and entertainment memorabilia

**An autograph is authenticated or it is decoration.** Name the authenticator —
PSA/DNA, JSA, Beckett, Roger Epperson for music — and the certificate number
where the lot gives one. A certificate of authenticity from the seller
authenticates nothing and is not a third-party opinion. Signed material without
named third-party authentication does not carry a price claim in an article.

**Signed is not inscribed.** A flat signature, a personalised inscription and a
signed-and-dated piece are different objects at different prices. Reproduce the
lot's wording.

**Prop, replica and production-made.** A screen-used prop, a production-made
piece that never appeared on camera, a crew gift and a licensed replica are four
markets. A replica is not a prop however well made, and the difference is
usually two orders of magnitude.

**Screen-matched is the strongest claim in the field.** Screen-used, screen-matched
and production-used are not synonyms: screen-matched means a specific frame has
been identified. If a lot claims a match, say so; if it only claims use, do not
upgrade it.

**Worn versus owned.** Stage-worn, tour-worn and simply owned are descending
claims. So are "played" and "owned" for instruments.

**Hammer versus price realised.** As everywhere: say which, take both figures
from the same page, never compare across the two.

## Access, as at 22 September 2026

Propstore, Premiere Props, Omega Auctions, Tracks, Guernsey's and WyCo answered
a plain fetch. VIP Fan Auctions, RR Auction and Iconic Auctions refused an
automated request and loaded in a browser session with nothing asked of anyone.
The Golden Closet served an active challenge and needs the operator; do not
attempt to solve it. Julien's refuses an automated request and was not reachable
from the browser pane in this session — treat it as untested at that rung. The ladder for a page that will not open: a
plain fetch, then the in-app browser, then the user's own Chrome, where their
logins are (the workspace `CLAUDE.md`). A challenge is never solved and a
credential is never typed; if the page still will not open, the figure does not
go in.
