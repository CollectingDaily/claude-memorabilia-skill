## Verification notes for comics

**Grade is the price.** A CGC 9.8 and a CGC 6.0 of the same issue can differ by
two orders of magnitude. Always state the grade and the grader alongside the
price, exactly as the lot gives them. A price without a grade is meaningless in
this field and must not be published.

**Restored, qualified and conserved grades are not the same as universal.** A
purple label (restored) or green label (qualified) copy sells far below a blue
label (universal) copy at the same numeric grade. Reproduce the label colour or
designation from the lot, never just the number.

**Census populations move.** "Only 12 known in 9.8" is true on the day you read
it and not afterwards. Give the figure with the date you checked it and name the
census, or leave it out.

**Raw versus slabbed.** An ungraded copy and a slabbed copy are different
markets. Do not compare them or average across them.

**Pence copies and UK variants** carry their own premiums and discounts and are
a genuine PFC-relevant niche. Note when a copy is a pence variant.
