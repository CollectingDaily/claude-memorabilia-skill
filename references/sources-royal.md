# Sources: royal memorabilia

Added on 3 November 2026 at the user's request. Read `sources-major-houses.md` and
`sources-press.md` alongside it, and `sources-autographs.md` for signed royal
letters and photographs.

**Scope.** Items of or belonging to royal families, royal letters, cards and
signed photographs, presentation pieces, clothing and possessions, and royal
commemoratives. Coins, stamps and medals have their own fields. A realised price
must come from a page you have fetched in this session.

## Verification notes

Royal provenance is the price: state the chain the lot gives (who owned it, how it
left the household). A commemorative made for sale to the public is not a royal
possession. Reeman Dansie prices are hammer; never set them beside a premium
total.
