# Sources: rare books

Added on 3 November 2026 at the user's request. Read `sources-major-houses.md` and
`sources-press.md` alongside it, and `sources-autographs.md` for signed and
inscribed copies.

**Scope.** Rare and antiquarian books, first editions, signed and inscribed
copies, fine bindings, illustrated and children's books, maps and atlases, and
manuscripts sold as books. A realised price must come from a page you have fetched
in this session.

## Primary authorised sources: realised prices

- **Swann Galleries**, https://www.swanngalleries.com/books-manuscripts/. Its home
  page links Books & Manuscripts with Americana, Autographs, Fine Books and Maps &
  Atlases (seen 3 November 2026); the department page has not been opened yet.
  Also on the art list.
- **Forum Auctions**, https://www.forumauctions.co.uk/departments/books-and-manuscripts.
  Read on 3 November 2026: antiquarian books, modern first editions, fine bindings,
  travel books, maps and atlases, children's and illustrated books and manuscripts.
  An unattended fetch returned 202 with no page; opened in the in-app browser.
- **Dominic Winter**, https://www.dominicwinter.co.uk/books. Its home page lists
  printed books sales on 3 November 2026 (November and December 2026). Opened in the
  in-app browser; the books page itself has not been opened yet.
- **Hansons**, https://hansonsauctioneers.co.uk/department/rare-book-auctions/.
  Read on 3 November 2026: "dedicated monthly sales of antiquarian books, modern
  firsts, manuscripts, autograph letters", run as timed online auctions for two
  weeks. Opened unattended.
- **Lyon & Turnbull**, https://www.lyonandturnbull.com/departments/books-manuscripts.
  Read on 3 November 2026: rare books, manuscripts and maps; the page states "All
  sale results shown are inclusive of buyer's premium." An unattended fetch got the
  heading only; opened in the in-app browser. Also on the art list.
- **University Archives**, https://www.universityarchives.com/. "Rare Autographs,
  Manuscripts, Books & Photographs." Its home page (read 3 November 2026) reports a
  $250,000 sale of a first edition of The Book of Mormon (1830) in its 18 February
  2026 auction, called a record for the title. Access: 403 to an unattended fetch;
  opened in the in-app browser.

## Dealer marketplace (sold prices only from its own sales articles)

- **AbeBooks**, https://www.abebooks.com/. A marketplace of independent sellers
  ("Shop from independent sellers"). **A listing is an asking price, never a sale
  price.** Its "Most Expensive Sales" articles and
  https://www.abebooks.com/books/rarebooks/30-years-of-extraordinary-sales give
  prices of books sold through the site (read 3 November 2026). Call them sales
  through AbeBooks, never auction results, and never set them beside a hammer or
  premium price as comparable. Opened in the in-app browser.

## Majors

- **Sotheby's**, https://www.sothebys.com/en/departments/books-manuscripts. Read on
  3 November 2026: books and manuscripts "from the Middle Ages to the 21st century".
- **Christie's, Bonhams and Heritage**: authorised; their book departments have not
  been opened yet.

## Verification notes

Edition and issue points are the price: state the edition, printing or issue, the
condition, whether the dust jacket is present, and any inscription, exactly as the
lot gives them. A book-club or later printing is not a first edition.
