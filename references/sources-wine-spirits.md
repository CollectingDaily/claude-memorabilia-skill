### Market data and investment platforms (context only, NEVER a realised price)
**These do not publish hammer prices.** Wine-Searcher lists merchant asking prices; Liv-ex publishes exchange indices and trade prices; CultX, WineCap, InVintory, WhiskyReturns and BAXUS are market, portfolio or valuation platforms. An asking price, an index level or a valuation is not a sale. Use them for market direction, index movement, relative scarcity and collecting trends, always attributed as such — never to support "sold for".

- Wine-Searcher — https://www.wine-searcher.com/
- Liv-ex — https://www.liv-ex.com/

### Verification notes for wine and spirits

**Per lot versus per bottle.** This is the single easiest way to publish a wrong number in this category. A lot is frequently a case of 6 or 12, an assortment, or a vertical spanning several vintages. Read the lot description and state which you mean. If a 12-bottle case made £30,000, that is £2,500 a bottle, and the two figures are not interchangeable. Record the lot composition in sources-log.md alongside the price.

**Bottle format changes the price.** Magnum, jeroboam, imperial and other large formats carry a premium that is not a simple multiple of the 75cl price. Never compare across formats without saying so. For spirits, note bottle size, ABV and whether the lot is a bottle or a cask — cask prices are not bottle prices.

**Access.** Several of these sites refuse automated requests, so the standing rule bites harder here than elsewhere: if you cannot fetch the page, you cannot cite it. As at 19 August 2026, Sotheby's, Bonhams, Hart Davis Hart, Zachys and Whisky-Online Auctions responded normally; Christie's, Acker Wines, Whisky Auctioneer, Heritage Auctions and Wine-Searcher refused automated clients; Whisky Quarterly did not respond at all. Where a house blocks the fetch, find the same sale reported on a fetchable source, or drop the item. Do not cite from memory or from a search snippet.

**Currency.** Wine and spirit sales are frequently in USD, HKD or EUR — Sotheby's and Acker in Hong Kong and New York, Whisky Auctioneer in GBP, Zachys and Hart Davis Hart in USD. Convert to British pounds at the rate on the sale date, as with every other article, and say nothing about a price you have not converted.
