# Sources: political memorabilia

Added on 3 November 2026 at the user's request. Read `sources-major-houses.md`
and `sources-press.md` alongside it, and `sources-autographs.md` for signed
material.

**Scope.** Campaign and election material (buttons, posters, ribbons, ephemera),
inaugural and office items, personal possessions of leaders, founding and
historical political documents, and signed political material. Royal-only items,
medals, stamps and coins are out. A realised price must come from a page you have
fetched in this session.

## Primary authorised sources: realised prices

- **Hake's Auctions**, https://www.hakes.com/. York, Pennsylvania. The specialist
  for American political Americana: campaign buttons, ribbons, posters, jugates.
  Also on the comics and toys lists. Access measured on 3 November 2026:
  `www.hakes.com` lot and catalogue pages open in the user's Chrome and show
  "Sold for" prices once a timed sale has ended; `payment.hakes.com` showed a
  Chrome error page. A current Premier sale shows a live bid, not a price. Hake's
  pages state no hammer-or-premium basis: record "not stated".
- **RR Auction**, https://www.rrauction.com/. Signed presidential letters,
  documents and artifacts. See `sources-autographs.md`.
- **Alexander Historical Auctions**, https://www.alexautographs.com/. Historic
  documents and Americana. Not yet opened.
- **Swann Galleries**, https://www.swanngalleries.com/. Printed and manuscript
  Americana. Unattended fetch returned 403 on 3 November 2026: read in the user's
  Chrome.
- **Weiss Auctions** (secondary), https://weissauctions.com/. Lynbrook, New York.
  A general-collectibles house. Its About page (read 3 November 2026) lists
  "Americana", "Autographs" and "Military" among its categories; it does not name
  political memorabilia. Use for occasional lots only. Whether its lot pages show
  realised prices is not yet checked.

## Majors

Heritage Auctions, Christie's, Sotheby's and Bonhams from
`sources-major-houses.md`. Heritage sold an 1864 Lincoln and Johnson jugate
poster for $250,000 on 2 November 2019 (read on its own lot page, 3 November
2026).

## Verification notes

Campaign items vary by size, variety and condition (a 1.25-inch and a 7/8-inch
button of the same ticket are different lots): state the size, the variety and
the grade the lot gives. A current bid is not a price and an estimate is not a
price. A lot page that shows only an estimate has no sold price, even after
signing in (a Bonhams lot, checked 3 November 2026).
